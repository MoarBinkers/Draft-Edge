Useful for pulling in mock drafts, a friend's league, or any draft you don't own. Find a draft ID in a Sleeper draft's URL. These are combined with anything found from your username above.

Enter a Sleeper username and this pulls every pick from every draft in your leagues for that season, then averages them out per player — real ADP, from drafts you've actually sat in.

The Board reflects Sleeper's search_rank field — the ranking Sleeper itself uses to order players in search and default draft boards. Sleeper refreshes it every so often, not live to the pick.

My ADP is calculated entirely from real pick data in the drafts you specify — no estimate, no third-party source, just the average pick number each player actually went at.

Everything on this page is fetched directly from Sleeper's public read-only API in your browser. Nothing is stored, tracked, or sent anywhere else. Not affiliated with Sleeper.
